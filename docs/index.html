Features

♞

Pikafish Engine

Pikafish, a leading xiangqi engine with NNUE evaluation, runs on the phone itself. Eight levels: Novice and Learner, for people who have just learnt the moves, draw among the engine's best few moves; from Beginner up the search runs from depth 3 to depth 20, and Grandmaster thinks ten seconds a move.

🎯

Endgame Puzzles

30 studies taken unaltered from the classical manuals, mates in 2 to 12, each checked by the engine. Retry, or step through the solution.

💡

Hint System

Stuck on a move? Tap the hint button and the AI will suggest the best play for your position.

🎨

Classical Design

A pale jade board on an ink-wash landscape, pieces in a brush-style Kai typeface in cinnabar red and ink black, and a smoky ink dark theme, Night Jade, that the app opens in.

💾

Save & Resume

Games auto-save when you leave. Come back anytime and pick up right where you left off.

📖

Game Replay

Step through your game from a bar under the board, or review it with a graph of how it went: drag along it, see the engine's move at every step, and jump between the mistakes a deeper search confirmed. The engine analyses the game while you think, so the review is ready almost at once.

The Engine

PartDetail
EnginePikafish, a Stockfish descendant built for xiangqi rules
EvaluationNNUE neural network
On the phoneNative arm64 binary over UCI, 2 threads, 32 MB hash
LevelsNovice and Learner draw among the six best moves by score, never missing a mate; from Beginner up only the search depth (or time) changes
FallbackA built-in alpha-beta search, used only if the engine cannot start

Privacy

This app does not collect your name, contact details or any other personal information. Everything except the optional “Play a friend online” works without an internet connection. An online game is stored in Google Firebase under an anonymous ID, readable only by the two players, and its room is deleted when the host cancels it or the last player leaves a finished game. No tracking, analytics, or advertisements. All other game data is stored locally on your device.

Read the full privacy policy, or see how to have an online game deleted.
